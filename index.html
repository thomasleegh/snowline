Where the numbers come from

  1. Snow: Open-Meteo weather model at each resort's mid-mountain elevation. 24h and 72h are rolling hourly sums; base is modeled snow depth. Snow level is the forecast freezing level minus ~1,000 ft. It's a model, not the resort's stake, so expect it to differ from the resort report.
  2. Drive: OSRM road routing from your origin (free-flow time) × your departure traffic factor + chain delay. Mammoth is routed via Minden, since Tioga closes in winter. The traffic factor is an estimate; no free live-traffic API exists.
  3. Chains: Caltrans CWWP2 chain-control feed for I-80, US-50, CA-88, CA-4 and CA-108. R1 adds ~15 min, R2 ~35, R3 ~60.
  4. Price: "listed" = adult weekday price listed on OnTheSnow for 2026-27; "est." = my estimate. Weekends and holidays cost more. No resort publishes a price feed.
  5. Confidence: compares the model's 72h snowfall with the measured snow-depth rise at the nearest NRCS SNOTEL station within 30 km. Agreement = High, same direction = Medium, disagreement or no station = Low. Fresh snow settles, so depth usually rises 60–90% of what fell.
  6. Open / closed: from each resort's announced or projected 2026-27 opening day, not live. A resort past its opening day counts as open; storms, low snow or closures can change that, so tap Lifts open before you drive. Open resorts sort to the top, not-yet-open to the bottom. Best bet only picks resorts open by your ski day; with no new snow anywhere, the shortest drive wins.
  7. Passes: 2025-26 lineups. Confirm 2026-27 before relying on them.

Worth-it score

  1. (24h × 2 + 72h × 2 + base ÷ 10) ÷ drive hours.
  2. Long drives cost extra: full credit up to 4 h, sliding to 25% at 7 h or more.
  3. Best bet only picks resorts with lifts running on your ski day (Saturday for Fri 3 pm / Sat 5 am, next weekday for Weekday AM): open now, or opening day on or before then.
  4. × 1.25 when a pass you hold covers the resort.
  5. Data caches for 30 minutes. Hit Refresh to pull new numbers.
